## Per defect

1. Rarity colours. Root cause: Common (0.82,0.82,0.80) was 15 units from the body text. Fix: one source, `ItemTooltip` (tooltip, slot headline, worn label all read `RarityColor`): Common cool slate (0.58,0.62,0.68), Uncommon (0.36,0.80,0.38), Rare blue (0.38,0.60,1.0). Gate: RGB distance from body text >= 0.2 and contrast >= 4.5:1 on panel and tooltip background for all three, Rare is blue (`inventoryui`). Seen: `r720/snapshots/a_loot_selected.png` Uncommon green title/rarity/affix line clearly distinct from white-cream text. Rare/Common are gate-verified only: no Rare or Common item dropped in my windowed runs.
2. Back slot. Root cause: the label text was replaced by `worn.displayName`; tooltip alpha 0.97 and clamped only to the panel so it covered slot rows. Fix: label is now two lines (dim slot name, item name in rarity colour), empty slots show the slot name (`SlotLabel`: Main hand, Off hand...); tooltip fully opaque (and panel alpha 1.0: the old 0.97 let a bright HDR scene show through); tooltip clamped to stay left of the equipment column, worn-slot tooltips sit beside the column. Seen: `r720/snapshots/c_worn_label.png` ("Back" over green "Emberthread Mantle", worn 1, panel solid), `e_back_tooltip.png` (tooltip x 532-690, column starts x 782, opaque). Before: `p4a/r720/snapshots/29_cloak_worn.png`.
3. Right-click equip. Root cause (code): `HandleRightClick` cancelled whenever any selection existed, so a leftover left-click selection swallowed the right click (consistent with P4a "selection was cleared"). The real mouse path itself was fine. Fix (revised after review M1): with a selection pending, a right click over an EQUIPPABLE bag item wears it; over any other item (consumable, coin, ammo) it stays "let go" via `CancelSelection()` (rebuilds), so it can never drink a draught. Gates (`inventoryui`): cloak, cuirass, boots, blade each with and without a selection pending; a selected Blood Draught and a selected coin right-clicked: units and health unchanged, selection cleared, no stale "Selected" hint, no highlight frame. Windowed: bridge right button on the Mantle cell with a selection pending wore it (`r720/snapshots/c_worn_label.png`). Only the Mantle (Back) was exercised through the real mouse; the other three are headless-only.
4. Stale HUD text. Root cause: "Satchel open" was raised on Open and never cleared; the prompt was only refreshed when the target changed; the hold bar was built full and visible. Fix: removed the Satchel status line (the panel is the feedback); `Interactor` re-reads the prompt each frame and re-raises on change, clears target and prompt when the run ends; hold bar and its back are hidden until a hold starts. Seen: no "Satchel open" in `r720/*` (before: `p4a/.../32_after_close_inv.png`), no yellow hold bar in `after720/snapshots/hud.png` (before: `before720/snapshots/after_descend.png`), prompt after opening reads "Chest (empty)  -  This chest is empty" (`after720/snapshots/chest_opened.png`). The lingering "Hit -15 HP" toast is the normal 2.6 s fade, not changed.
5. Satchel under the death screen. Root cause: `InventoryRoot` is not under `HudRoot`, so hiding the HUD left it open. Fix: `InventoryScreenController` closes (without relocking the cursor, the result screen needs it) on Dead/Extracted/Lobby, refuses to open after the run ended, `HandlePointer` also self-closes; tooltip/hover/selection cleared. Seen: `after720/snapshots/death_with_satchel_open.png` is a death screen with no satchel, taken when the satchel was open (empty bag, Loot value 0 / Items 0 on that shot; the run on the first build `after/`). A second death with a filled bag happened on `after2` but that PNG was overwritten, so a filled-bag windowed shot does not exist. Before: `p4a/.../40_mantle_tip.png`. Gate: `death` flow opens the satchel, dies, asserts closed (weak: opened with no selection/hover, and the post-death click check is tautological; strengthening is an open follow-up).
6. Layout. Root causes: carry/hint rectangle only 32 px tall for 2-3 lines; panel bottom (y 555 at 720p) ran under the skill cards (top y 514); lobby cards 420 px tall ended at y 630 under DESCEND (y 599). Fix: hint font 13, detail 56 px / hint band 74 px (column sum unchanged), shorter hint text using tooltip-style fixed lines instead of raw modifier text (also removes "Armor +2,00"); panel lifted 46 px (bottom y 509 at 720p); lobby cards 380 px, 36 px below centre (y 206..586). Seen at 1280x720: `after720/snapshots/lobby.png` (cards end above DESCEND), `inv_empty.png` (hint on one line, panel clears Dash card), at 1920x1080: `r1080/snapshots/lobby_1080.png`, `inv_empty_1080.png` (all inside window, hint complete). Gate: hint text height <= its rectangle (idle and with selection) and rectangle inside the panel.
7. Text. (a) MP cost twice: `SkillData.ShortSummary` already contains "24 MP | 7s", HUD prefixed it again; now Summary only (`hud.png`: "24 MP  |  7s cooldown"). (b) "Hold to open chest (hold)": `Interactor.BuildPrompt` appends "(hold)" only when the label lacks "hold" (chest prompt seen: `after720/snapshots/chest_done3.png` "Hold to open chest"); gate with a stub IInteractable. (c) hotbar wrap: label best-fit 8-12 pt in a 66 px box, wraps on words (seen: `r720/snapshots/a_loot_selected.png` bottom: "Emberthread / Mantle"). (d) affix names: `AffixStatKeys.DisplayName` / `DisplayLine` in `AffixVocabulary.cs` for all 13 keys, "Armour", signs ("+3 Strength", "-2 Strength", never "+-", "+0.2/s Mana regen"); tooltip shows `RolledDisplayLines`, `RolledLines`/`ToPlainText` keep canonical keys (existing CloakSlot/inventoryui assertions on "+4 armor" unchanged); fixed-modifier lines no longer print "+-". Seen: "+0.09 Move speed". Gate loops every enum key (13, equals `KeyCount`), no underscore. UID is only the muted 8-char short id. The skill key glyphs "A"/"П" are the user's keyboard layout via `GetBindingDisplayString` (F/G on a Cyrillic layout), not a bug.
8. Tooltip after tab round trip. `_hoverInstance/_hoverDefinition` are now reset when switching back to the Bag tab, so the next frame re-shows it without a pointer move. Windowed: Character tab and back worked (`d_character_tab.png`), but the pointer necessarily rests on the tab button, so a no-move return could not be demonstrated visually; no headless gate for it (the logic is a two-line reset).
9. Enemy readability. (a) Small yellow bar over distant enemies = torch sconce: `EmberdelveLevelBuilder.BuildTorches` put each emissive 0.18x0.42x0.18 box at the cell centre, floating in the corridor. Fix: `TorchFlicker.Start` mounts the "Sconce" child on the nearest wall (raycast on the level layer only, capped at 6 m; the light does not move, no collider involved). Gate: all 10 sconces touch a wall. Seen: `after720/snapshots/hud.png` (bar gone, sconce on right wall) vs `before720/snapshots/after_descend.png`. This does NOT explain the screen-filling quads in `p4a/21_pre_fight.png` / `22_swing.png` (sconces hang at 2.3-2.6 m, the eye is at 1.66 m). The telegraph wedge is also not the cause (headless: flat, 0.00 m high, footprint <= 4.95 m). (b) Screen-filling quads: reproduced on my own build at contact range (`r720b/snapshots/p2.png`, `p3.png`: enemy body and head face blown out to pale yellow/white, edges at the body-top/head heights, with my brighter enemy material making it worse). Cause: the hand torch (`Player_Torch`, intensity 9, 0.25 m in front of the camera) is inverse-square blown out on any surface the player faces at contact. Fix: `TorchFlicker` on the hand torch (parent is a Camera) scales intensity within 2.5 m of what the camera faces (sphere cast) so the lit surface never receives more light than at 2.5 m, smoothed; far away it is unchanged. Gate (`enemyvisual`): enemy at 0.9 m -> scale < 0.4, enemy at 12 m -> scale > 0.9. Seen on the final build: `rfinal/snapshots/q4.png` (Warden at contact on the left edge is a plain orange face, not white; Hollow Servitor slain) vs `r720b/snapshots/p3.png` (same scenario before the fix). A large body face at 0.7 m still fills a lot of the view; that is geometry, not an artefact. I did not get a clean frontal shot with the enemy centred at 1 m. (c) Enemy body: `Enemy_Body.mat` (0.23,0.22,0.26) -> (0.40,0.36,0.42) emissive (0.10,0.06,0.09); `Enemy_EliteBody.mat` -> (0.50,0.22,0.18) emissive (0.30,0.08,0.04) (`EmberdelveMaterials.cs` constants updated for fresh builds). Seen clearly at 5 m and melee (`after720/snapshots/c1.png`, `melee6.png`) vs near-black before. The 3 room-centre torches' sconces were only moved after the first windowed builds; gate-verified, and the final build `final/` includes them.
