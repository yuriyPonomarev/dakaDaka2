## Design
- `Runtime/Combat/CombatKit.cs` (new): `KitSeedReason` (ClassApply, Respawn, Death, Revision, Weapon), `KitSeed` / `KitAmmoSeed` (what a rig may report), `KitView` (read-only picture), `IKitSeedSource` / `IKitView` (rig side), and the internal plain-C# record `CombatKitState`: kit epoch, active-kit snapshot, open cast id, mana (current, max, regen, delay, since-spend, regen allowed), per-weapon ammo pools (magazine, reserve, max reserve, magazine size, reload time), active weapon id, reload state and timer, frozen flag (dead). Regen and reload arithmetic is the old `ManaComponent.Update` / `RangedCombat.FinishReload` arithmetic, moved.
- `Runtime/Combat/LocalCombatAuthority.Kit.cs` (new partial): records keyed by combat id; `SeedKit(sender, combatId, seed)` (the one seeding API, sender-owned), `TryGetKit`, `TryGetSkillCooldown`, `TryStartReload`, `TryForfeitRound`; `TickKits()` from `LateUpdate` on `Now` (dt clamped to 0..0.5 s per frame so a hitch or a restored clock cannot refill a pool) and `AdvanceKitsTo(time)` (internal, exact, for injected-clock tests); every tick pushes the record into the entity's `IKitView`, changed or not, so an edited display is put back; trusted-host internals (`HostGrantReserve`, `HostCanAcceptReserve`, `HostEditKit` for fixtures); `KitAccess`, the one place validation reads mana/ammo/kit/epoch: the record when the entity has one, the R1b `ISkillCasterState`/`IAmmoSource` otherwise (fixtures such as the SkillAuthority gate's fake casters have no record and behave exactly as before). First contact with an `IKitSeedSource` that was never seeded adopts what it has once.
- Commands validate and spend against the record: `TryBeginSkillCast` (kit list, epoch, mana, ammo; open cast noted), `SubmitAttack` and `LaunchProjectile` (ammo). Cooldown stamps stay in the authority and are cleared on ClassApply/Respawn/Death; `TryGetSkillCooldown` exposes them.
- Seeding points (all through `PlayerRig.SeedKit(reason)`, which sends as the rig's sender):
  - ClassApply: end of `PlayerRig.ApplyClass` (the interim weapon/vitals seeds inside `ClassLoadout.Apply` are suppressed). New epoch, pools rebuilt and refilled, ammo pools rebuilt from the loadout. This is the only refill.
  - Death: `SkillCaster.HandleDeath`. New epoch, regen and reload suspended, values untouched.
  - Respawn: `SkillCaster.HandleKitRestored`. New epoch, regen resumes (spend clock reset as `SetRegenerationActive(true)` did), mana and ammo carried, not refilled (same as before).
  - Revision: `PlayerRig.RefreshEquipmentStats` (equipment/loadout revision). Mana ceiling and regen only, clamp-only exactly like `SetMax`/`SetRegen`; a seed can never raise the pool (no free refill, no doubled bonus: the R10b effective-stat path is untouched, only its result is copied into the record).
  - Weapon: `RangedCombat.Initialize` (called on every equip). Pools the authority already holds win over the seed values (a swap is not a refill, caps are updated); only a never-seen pool is adopted; a reload in progress ends (as `Initialize` always did).
- Rig side (display only): `PlayerRig` implements `IKitView`/`IKitSeedSource` and exposes `TryGetAuthorityKit`; `ManaComponent.ApplyView` makes the pool a mirror (no self regeneration once bound; `IsAuthorityView`, `RegenDelay` added); `RangedCombat.ApplyKitView` mirrors magazine, reserve, reload flag and timer (reload start is now `ICombatAuthority.TryStartReload`; a refused shot still costs its round through `TryForfeitRound`; `AddAmmo` is now internal and goes to the record via `HostGrantReserve`; `MaxReserve` internal); `SkillCaster` takes its cooldown display from `TryGetSkillCooldown` and seeds Death/Respawn. `ClassLoadout.CaptureAmmoSeeds` builds the per-weapon pool list. An unseeded rig (no authority in the scene) keeps the old self-ticking behavior.
- `ICombatAuthority` gained `SeedKit`, `TryGetKit`, `TryGetSkillCooldown`, `TryStartReload`, `TryForfeitRound` (only implementer: LocalCombatAuthority).

## Gate literal / fixture changes (old -> new, reason)
No expected number was changed. Fixture call sites that edited the rig's components directly now edit the authority record through `EmberdelveKitFixtures` (falls back to the direct call when the rig has no record), because a direct edit is by design a forgery that the authority ignores and overwrites:
- `rig.Mana.RefillToMax()` -> `EmberdelveKitFixtures.RefillMana(rig)` (AttributeSkill x12, SkillAuthority x2, SenderOwnership).
- `rig.Mana.TrySpend(n)` / `mana.TrySpend(n)` -> `EmberdelveKitFixtures.SpendMana(mana, n)` (AttributeVitals x3, NumericAffix, RolledAttributes, SmokeTest x4); `mana.Restore(x)` -> `RestoreMana` (SmokeTest x2).
- `ranged.RefundAmmo(n)` -> `RefundAmmo` (AttributeAction x2, SmokeTest, SkillAuthority, SenderOwnership); `ranged.TryConsumeAmmo(n)` -> `ConsumeAmmo` (CombatFlow x2, SmokeTest, SkillAuthority).
- `ranged.Initialize(weapon, reserve, max, mag)` used as "refill" -> `ResetAmmo(...)` (AttributeSkill x3, InventoryAuthority, SmokeTest): Initialize no longer refills a known pool; the fixture also sets the record.
`ranged.AddAmmo` calls (CombatFlow, InventoryAuthority) are unchanged: the production path already routes to the record.

## New gate: `Editor/EmberdelveKitStateGate.cs` (guid in its .meta), `[Emberdelve][KitState] PASS|FAIL checks=N failures=M`, about 69 check sites (not run)
Record and display equal after join; forged `Mana.Restore/SetMax` on the rig cannot pay for a cast (refused against the record, record unchanged, forged values overwritten on the next push); forged `RefundAmmo` + `Initialize(full)` on the rig cannot buy a shot or a Multi-shot (refused, record 0, display reset); regen proven on an injected clock (frames do not regenerate, half delay no regen, past delay regen x dt exact); reload on the injected clock (start refused for full magazine / other player, half time progress 0.5, finish moves the right rounds); cooldown display equals the authority; death/respawn epochs, regen flags, no refill on respawn, old cooldown dies with the epoch; equipment Revision seeds are clamp-only (ceiling up does not pay out, down clamps, back up does not refill); weapon swap keeps a spent crossbow round, a forged Weapon seed with a full magazine does not refill; class apply resets epoch, mana, magazine and reserve from the class; other player / host / no sender cannot seed.

## Risks
- Largest slice: two sources of truth still exist in the transition (rig components are views but still have local mutators); any gate or tool not found by my grep that edits `Mana`/`RangedCombat` directly on a seeded rig will see its edit overwritten. Fixtures list above is from a text search, not from running.
- Gates that inject a clock (`SetClock`) now also drive mana regen and reload for real rigs; a stuck injected clock means no regen/reload during that gate. A restored real clock after a large injected value is clamped to 0.5 s per frame so it cannot refill pools.
- Regen and reload tick in `LateUpdate` instead of `Update` (one frame later in the same frame order); HUD events still fire per change.
- Weapon/Revision/first-contact seeds are rig-reported: a client that owns the rig can still lie at a never-seen weapon pool or at ClassApply (a run start). Closing that needs the class/weapon asset on the authority side (U3), not a rig report.
- Skill cooldown display now follows the authority stamp (cast time on the authority clock), the local countdown remains for unseeded rigs; the authority's 0.05 s cast tolerance means the display can read 0.05 s while a cast would already pass.
- `CompleteSkillCast` is still caller-driven and the open-cast id is informational (no new one-cast-at-a-time refusal); both are R1c3. Dash is untouched (R1c3).
- `RangedCombat.AddAmmo` is now internal; any external caller outside the project assembly would not compile.
- The gate assumes the warrior class has mana regen > 0, the bow a non-zero reload time and reserve > 0, and that `ClassLoadout.SwitchToSlot` is not locked in the fixture; it applies `ApplyClass` once more to a live spawned archer (re-mints starting items, which may log a bag-full message).
