# D1a: swept weapon melee, hit volumes, hit locations, wall stops (offline, authority-owned)

Product constraint: the game plays like Dark and Darker (own names, art, numbers). This slice replaces the arc/overlap melee with a weapon SEGMENT that sweeps through per-entity hit volumes along an authored path, owned and stepped by the combat authority. Skills (R1b), ranged, enemies' claw attack and the R1a/R1b authority rules are untouched. Nothing is committed.

## Verified starting point (HEAD ce36eb7d)
- `MeleeCombat.EnterActive` scanned with `HitScanner.CollectMeleeHits` (overlap sphere + 3D angle arc + LOS to the target centre), picked the closest candidate and sent ONE `SubmitAttack` with a client-chosen hit point.
- `LocalCombatAuthority.SubmitAttack` accepted a melee hit up to about 4.2 m (reach + hitSphere + tolerance 0.8 + body radius), no arc, no walls (R1a review H2). Headshot = claimed point on the target's head collider (R1a review H1).
- Player had no head collider and no body parts; enemies had one body box and one head box (physics colliders).

## Design

### 1. Swing patterns as data (`Runtime/Data/SwingPatternData.cs`, ScriptableObject)
A pattern is the full motion of one melee attack in the ATTACKER'S AIM FRAME (origin = the eye, +Z = view direction, +X right, +Y up, `Quaternion.Euler(pitch, yaw, 0)`, no roll).
- `SwingKind`: HorizontalLeftToRight, HorizontalRightToLeft, Overhead, Thrust.
- Timing (authority clock, seconds): `windupTime`, `activeTime`, `recoveryTime`, `wallRecoilTime`. Only the ACTIVE phase deals damage; windup and recovery are the same data eased from / to a rest pose, so the visual has one source.
- Path: `SwingKey[] keys` = {t (0..1 over the active phase), basePos (hand, metres in the aim frame), bladeYaw, bladePitch (degrees, yaw right-positive, pitch DOWN-positive)}. Linear interpolation between keys, so the curve is exactly the data. `Evaluate(swingTime)` (all three phases) and `ActivePose(u)`.
- Segment: base = `basePos`, tip = base + direction x `BladeLength(weapon)`, `BladeLength = EffectiveReach - sweepRadius - reachInset`, `EffectiveReach = (weapon.reach + weapon.hitSphereRadius) * reachScale`. So the weapon's reach numbers come from the weapon asset: after the H1 retune (review round 1) the sword is (2.3 + 0.6) x 0.79 = 2.29 m from the eye to the farthest point the sweep can touch with a 1.2 m damage segment held at arm's length (it was 2.9 m with a 2.44 m segment), the rapier thrust is (2.7 + 0.45) x 0.74 = 2.33 m with a 1.15 m segment (was 2.71 m). `Weapon_AshenShortSword.activeWindow` is now 0.30 s (was 0.14 s).
- `sweepRadius` (added to every hit volume's radius), `contactWindow`, `maxTargets` (cleave limit), `damageMultiplier` (per pattern; D1b combo steps author it), `moveSpeedMultiplier` (HOOK for D1b, left at 1, not read by movement), `trailMaterial` (asset reference for the visual), rest pose.
- `WeaponData.swingPatterns[]` (step 0 patterns) and `TryResolveSwingPattern(comboStep, patternIndex, out pattern)`: the combo-step seam. Only step 0 exists; any other step is refused, so D1b adds data behind this one function. An empty list = legacy arc melee, which only enemies still use (`UsesSweptMelee`).

Authored by `EmberdelveAssetLibrary.ApplySwingPatterns` (idempotent, code-authoritative, called from `BuildAll`/`BuildDataOnly`): assets `Data/Pattern_SwordSlashLR/RL/Overhead`, `Pattern_RapierThrust`, `HitLocationTable`.

### 2. The authority owns the sweep (`Runtime/Combat/LocalCombatAuthority.Swings.cs`, partial class)
**Request** (plain data, `MeleeSwingIntent`): instigator id, weapon id, pattern index, combo step, aim yaw/pitch snapshot, sequence. There is no hit point, range, timestamp, damage, head flag or target field (the gate pins the field list by reflection).

**`TryBeginMeleeSwing`** validates, in this order, each with its own reason string: unknown instigator; dead; weapon provider / weapon null; claimed weapon id != equipped (R1a rule, strict); weapon has no swing patterns (ranged weapon, enemy claw); instigator has no `IAimPoseProvider`; (combo step, pattern index) not allowed by the weapon; stale/repeated sequence (same per-entity sequence as attacks); a swing already running; cooldown on the authority clock (`Now`, same `_lastAttackTime` stamp as weapon attacks, `cooldown x cooldownGrace`); aim snapshot within 12 degrees of the pose the authority holds (NaN refused). Only an accepted begin spends the cooldown. The snapshot is compared, never used to place the sweep.

**Step** (`StepSwings`, every `FixedUpdate`, or by hand in a gate on an injected clock; idempotent for an unchanged clock): the ACTIVE phase is cut into K equal time samples, K = `SwingGeometry.SampleCount(pattern, weapon)`, a pure function of the data (consecutive sampled segments are at most clamp(sweepRadius/2, 0.02, 0.06) m apart at their farthest point; sword slash K = 66 after the retune). Every sample whose time has been reached is processed in order, with the segment built from the pattern pose at that sample time in the attacker's AUTHORITATIVE aim frame (`IAimPoseProvider`: eye + yaw/pitch, interpolated between this tick's pose and the last one). Because the grid is fixed by the data, the result does not depend on the tick length (gate: 1 ms .. 2.5 s steps give identical hits, locations, samples and wall stop).

Per sample:
1. **Wall**: level geometry (`config.levelMask`, the LOS mask) is tested along the segment (`Linecast`) and at its base (`CheckSphere`). A wall ALWAYS clips the segment where it meets it (nothing is struck through a wall). The swing STOPS (recoil, `SwingEndReason.WallStop`, `wallRecoilTime`) when the blade point is moving INTO the surface (the pattern's own velocity against the surface normal); a blade that touches a wall while moving away from it or along it is clipped but keeps going. This rule came from the windowed run (see Findings): with "any contact stops" a slash that starts pointing at a corridor side wall ended on its first sample. A base buried in a wall clips the segment to nothing and always stops the swing.
2. **Targets**: every live, hostile, not-yet-hit entity that exposes `IHitShapeProvider` (and whose GameObject is active) is tested, segment against each hit capsule (segment distance <= volume radius + sweepRadius). The first touch opens a contact; volumes touched within `contactWindow` of it are compared and the DEEPEST (tie: Head, Body, Arms, Hands, Legs, Feet) decides the location. When the window closes (or the swing ends or the wall stops it) the hit is applied. Each target is hit at most once per swing; at most `maxTargets` targets per swing (cleave, in sweep order).
3. **Damage** goes through `DamageResolver.ResolveSwing`: (weapon base + flat affix bonus) x pattern multiplier x the instigator's outgoing multiplier x location multiplier, then the existing guard (`IDefenseProvider`, directional) and armour (`IArmorRatingProvider`) in `Finalize`. `DamageInfo.Location` carries the location; `Headshot` is true exactly for Head. `AcceptedCount` counts accepted hits; `GameEvents.RaiseDamageDealt/PlayerDamaged` fire as before.

`MeleeSwingStatus` (`TryGetMeleeSwingStatus`): phase (Windup, Active, Recovery, Finished), end reason (Completed, WallStop, InstigatorDied, InstigatorGone), elapsed, samples processed / total, wall contact sample, hits (target, location, amount, blocked, first sample, resolve sample, point). A dead or vanished instigator ends the swing and deals nothing afterwards.

**Legacy path closed for the player.** `SubmitAttack` now refuses a MELEE weapon attack from an entity that has a swing pose (`IAimPoseProvider`, i.e. the player) with "melee attacks from this instigator must begin a swept swing". That closes the reach slack and the no-arc/no-wall hole of the R1a review (H2). Enemies have no swing pose and keep their committed-wedge claw attack unchanged. `internal bool AllowLegacyMeleeAttacks` (Editor assembly via InternalsVisibleTo, default false) lets fixtures that probe the `SubmitAttack` claim rules with the sword opt in explicitly.

### 3. Hit volumes and locations
- `HitVolume` (MonoBehaviour: location, local capsule A/B + radius) and `HitShape` (plain world capsule) in `Runtime/Combat/HitVolume.cs`; `IHitShapeProvider.CollectHitShapes`. `HitVolumeLayout.EnsureHumanoid(root, scale, headHeight)` builds head, torso, two arms, two legs, two feet as children of a `HitVolumes` container (layer Ignore Raycast).
- **Deviation from the brief, on purpose:** the volumes are analytic shapes, NOT Colliders. They have no layer, join no physics query, so they cannot disturb the CharacterController, loot/interaction raycasts or the existing Body/Head colliders (still used by ranged rays and movement), and the sweep needs no physics scene (server-runnable). `OnDrawGizmosSelected` draws them.
- Layout (metres, local to the root, scale 1 = the enemy; player scale 0.92 with head centre at 1.68): head sphere r 0.24 at 1.85; torso capsule 1.00-1.45 r 0.33; arms +-0.43 x, 1.05-1.45, r 0.10; legs +-0.16 x, 0.50-0.80, r 0.13; feet +-0.16 x, 0.12-0.25, r 0.12. `PlayerRig` (WireRig) and `EnemyAgent` (Awake, elite scale 1.25) create them at runtime exactly like the rig's other ensured components, so no scene was rebuilt (the Level01 and Lobby scenes are unchanged). Crouch does not move the player's volumes yet.
- Location table (`HitLocationTable`, `GameConfig.hitLocations`, code default when empty): head 1.5, body 1.0, arms 0.8, hands 0.7, legs 0.6, feet 0.5 (hands have no volume yet). Our own tunables (DND_PARITY C9: same table shape, our own numbers). The head value is additive-capable: `Multiplier(Head, bonus)` = min(1.8, head + bonus); 1.8 is the existing `DamageResolver.HeadshotMultiplier` ceiling, kept as the cap. The client-claimed headshot flag is not consulted by melee at all.
- Ranged was NOT migrated: its headshot is still the R1a geometry rule (claimed point on the head collider, x1.8) and many gates pin x1.8. Listed below.

### 4. Thin client and visual
- `MeleeCombat` no longer scans or overlaps. On the primary press (still through the `EmberdelveInput` overrides) it builds a `MeleeSwingIntent` (sequence from `CombatRegistry.NextSequence`, aim snapshot from `rig.TryGetAimPose`), calls the authority, and only on acceptance plays the visual and runs its local windup/active/recovery timers (animation, `IsSwinging` for the guard rule, `CooldownRemaining` for the HUD, unchanged semantics). It polls `TryGetMeleeSwingStatus` and enters its recoil (`RecoilStarted`) when the authority reports a wall stop. Pattern choice (`ChoosePatternIndex`): sprint held = overhead (when the weapon has one), else the horizontal slashes alternate L->R, R->L, else the thrust; D1b replaces this with chains. `AttackStarted` now carries the `MeleeSwingIntent`; the unused `SwingResolved` event was removed. Guard/block code is unchanged.
- `WeaponVisualRig` (first person) is posed every frame by `SwingPatternData.Evaluate` in the world frame of the camera (the same frame the authority sweeps in), for windup, active and recovery. A thin bar "Sweep_Edge" is drawn along the exact damage segment (the blockout model is only 0.8 m long) and a ribbon (`SwingTrail`) is left in the world along the outer third of the segment for about a second after the active phase, built from the visual's own poses. `PlayRecoil` rebounds to rest on a wall stop. The material is a project asset (`EmberGate_Glow`) referenced from the pattern so the shader is in the build (my first build created a runtime material and drew a grey slab; see PNGs). Enemy telegraph wedge: untouched.

## Findings and open follow-ups
Recorded from review round 1 (not done here):
- **H2 turn rate (needed before networking)**: the sweep follows the live camera, so a flick inside one tick spreads the samples over the rotation and the "tick independent / no tunnelling" claim holds for a still or slowly turning camera only. Cap the per-tick yaw/pitch delta the authority accepts during a swing (for example 720 deg/s), add samples by angular delta, and pin it with a gate (a 180 degree flick in one tick must not hit a target behind). On a real server the pose is client input, so this is the main melee cheat surface.
- **M-b**: the sample spacing guarantee is an average over time-equal samples; multi-key D1b patterns with a fast segment need arc-length spacing (or the per-probe maximum).
- **M-c**: the authority accepts a swing while the client is "guarding" (guard is client-owned); refuse a begin while guarding and drop the guard during a swing.
- Chests and the Ember Gate pillars are on the interact layer and do not stop swings; no wall-impact feedback (spark, sound, camera kick); the visual blade/ribbon is not clipped at the wall while the damage segment is.
- Swing status could expose the live hit list to the HUD (hit location cue); there is no gate over `CombatRegistry` that every hostile entity exposes hit shapes, and none that every player-usable melee weapon (class weapons, main-hand `grantedWeapon`) has swing patterns (a melee weapon without patterns cannot attack at all now).
- The deepest-volume-in-a-short-window location rule is a deliberate deviation from "first volume touched" (it stops the arm from shielding the torso); record it in DND_PARITY. Pattern timings are duplicated from the weapon asset by the builder (two sources); make the pattern authoritative and derive the weapon display from it.
- The overhead on sprint is still a placeholder input; the rapier needs a slash step in D1b; the move-speed penalty hook is still unread, so the player swings at full run speed.
- **Enemies are not on the swept system (D1a-2)**: the claw attack keeps its committed-direction wedge telegraph and `SubmitAttack`; migrating enemies needs an `IAimPoseProvider` for the brain, per-enemy patterns, and a telegraph drawn from the pattern. It did not fall out cleanly (the wedge, the off-axis rule M1 of the R1a review and the brain's windup are all arc based), so it is not done.
- **Ranged hit locations**: ranged still uses the claimed-point-on-head-collider rule and x1.8; moving it to the location table (head 1.5) changes damage numbers pinned by CombatAuthority, SkillAuthority, AttributeAction and AttributeSkill gates. D2 (projectiles) is the place.
- **D1b**: combo chains (3 steps, per-step pattern and multiplier through `TryResolveSwingPattern`), impact zones (tip vs base), hit slow, `moveSpeedMultiplier` consumption, stamina-free block and guard break, pattern choice by input (sprint attack is a placeholder), hands volumes.
- Melee reach was cut to 2.3 m in review round 1 (it was 2.9 m); in tight corridors the blade can still touch walls. With the moving-into rule only an approaching blade stops, but a 3 m corridor still ends many slashes early, which is the intended Dark and Darker-like consequence and a tuning topic (`sweepRadius`, `reachScale`, key yaw range).
- Contact window: the damage lands up to `contactWindow` (16-20 ms, about 14 percent of the sweep) after the first touch so the deepest volume decides the location; location is therefore the best of a short window, not strictly the first volume touched.
- The pose is read from the camera on the authority's tick (local seam): a real server needs the replicated pose and the same sample grid; target volumes are read at the tick time (no lag compensation, no rewind of the targets' pose).
- Crouch does not move player hit volumes; the volumes are a first proportions pass.
- The visual shows the damage segment as a bar and ribbon because the blockout sword is 0.8 m while the segment is 2.4 m; a real model should be as long as the segment or hide the bar.
- Sprint selecting the overhead conflicts with nothing yet but is a placeholder input rule.
- `HitScanner.CollectMeleeHits` and the 3D-angle arc quirk (P4b F4) no longer exist; `WeaponData.arcAngle` remains for enemies and the lobby card.
