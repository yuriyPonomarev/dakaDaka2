## Verdict
Confirmed code-level geometry flaw: the player's melee arc is measured in 3D from eye height against a fixed 1.15 m center, so at contact range the 0.54 m height drop consumes ~41° of the sword's 60° half-arc and a modest upward camera pitch (or aiming at the tall elite's head) makes a touching enemy unhitable while the enemy's own flattened rule always lands. Not confirmed as the cause of this specific run: that needs the camera pitch/aim at the swing instants. Equally consistent, and cheaper: only ~2 of the 4 clicks became swings, and guard-held clicks are dropped silently.
