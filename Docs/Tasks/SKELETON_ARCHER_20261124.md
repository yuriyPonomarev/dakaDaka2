# Skeleton archer (Hollow Bowman) - 24 Nov 2026

User request: "make a skeleton archer" (together with restoring raid monsters, done in 572b24e98).

## Behaviour

- **Hollow Bowman** (`Enemy_HollowBowman`, id `hollow_bowman`): 55 HP, enemy-only **Bone Bow** (`Weapon_BoneBow`, id `bone_bow`: 16 Pierce per arrow, 32 m range, 30 m/s, gravity 9.81, no falloff, authority cooldown 2 s -> floor 1.6 s). Brain cooldown 2.6 s, draw (windup) 1.1 s, sight 22 m / 140 deg, hearing 9 m, chase 2.4 m/s.
- Tactics (`EnemyBrain.TickRangedChase/TickRangedAttack`): keeps a 6-14 m band with line of sight; closer than 3.5 m it backs off (retreat search over 7 directions, nav + wall checked); cornered, it shoots anyway; strafes while its cooldown runs; beyond the band it closes in. No melee (as the base Dark and Darker archer). It stands still while drawing; the draw tracks the target; losing the target or the line breaks the draw with no shot.
- The shot (`EnemyAgent.TryShootArrow`): one `AttackIntent` through the same validated `ICombatAuthority.LaunchProjectile` the player's bow uses (alive, ranged weapon in hand, sequence, cooldown on the authority clock, origin at its eye, direction equal to its own aim pose within 1 deg, muzzle not in a wall). The aim pose is yaw to the target's chest at release and the flatter ballistic pitch for the arrow's drop (`EnemyAgent.TryBallisticPitch`, zero gravity when the authority does not simulate ballistics). Flight, hit location, shields, walls and damage are the authority's.
- Telegraph: the visible draw (bow raised, string pulled to the cheek) plus a narrow ground strip (+-2.5 deg) along the aim whose length follows the target.
- Spawning: two Bowman records are appended to `DungeonLayout_AshenHalls` (5 Servitors, 1 Warden, 2 Bowmen). Floors 2-3 create them via `FloorContentSpawner`; on floor 1 the modular assembler now creates layout records beyond the scene's authored monsters on the simulating process (same `EnemyAgent.CreateForFloor` path), so the content-ready count still holds. Visual variant choice is shared: `EnemyData.VisualVariantFor`.
- Loot: own table `Loot_Enemy_HollowBowman` (the Servitor's coin/draught/arrow items, 2 rolls, 6 empty weight).
- Fix found by the new tests: `EnemyBrain.TickAttack` now clears the windup when the target dies or leaves mid-windup (an archer stayed drawn, a melee telegraph stayed lit until the next attack).

## Visual

- Variant `Archer` (male undead body, Idle_Loop / Walk_Loop / Jog_Fwd_Loop / Hit_Chest / Death01) with `EnemyBowRig`: the procedural recurve bow (`BowPresentation`, the player's bow geometry, x1.3 = a 1.4 m longbow) built at runtime under the left hand. Carried upright at the side at rest; raised to the shooting grip during the attack.
- Attack clip generated by `EmberdelveEnemyArcherClip` (no new third-party asset; Idle body, both arms solved in muscle space): raise and nock until 0.20, draw to the cheek by 0.45 (held through the windup), release 0.45-0.50 with a hand flick, hold to 0.62, lower by 0.92. The right hand is solved onto the bow's nock every frame (hand-to-nock 1-2 mm while drawn; worst IK residual 2.4 cm during the raise). Body yaw is the Idle's for the whole clip.
- Server driver and client proxy drive the clip and the bow's raise/draw from the same presentation clock (`EnemyAttackPhase.ArcherNormalizedTime/ArcherBowRaise/ArcherBowDraw`); release plays the bow sound.

## Review (Opus, read-only) and fixes

- Clients loaded the Minion body for the archer (`NetEnemyViews.LoadVisual` clamped kinds to CinderWarden): now clamps on `MaxVisualKind`; test `ProxyLoadsTheArcherVisualForTheArcherKind`.
- A target switch mid-draw could loose the arrow at the new target (even behind the archer): the draw is now bound to the target it started on (a different target at release looses nothing), and `TryShootArrow` refuses an aim more than 40 deg off the archer's facing.
- A faster player staying inside 3.5 m kept the archer fleeing forever: after `EnemyBrain.ArcherFleeBudget` (1.5 s) of fleeing it draws at point blank; test `AFollowingTargetCannotKeepTheArcherFromShootingForever`.
- Clients heard the release twice: the proxy no longer plays a sound for the archer's phase 2 (the arrow's shot event does).
- Mirror regression found in the first run (NetCombat/NetMovement/NetFinal: monsters in scenes that author none): the assembler now completes only scenes that author monsters, under their own (possibly deactivated) root.
- Smoke melee fixtures picked the nearest monster, which could be a retreating archer: the smoke's monster pickers skip archers.
- Gate: the behind-wall flow now places the player out of earshot behind a 40 m wall (the first version let the archer legitimately walk around a 6 m wall); the back-off flow was removed (the sweep gate's spawn point is outside the nav grid, so no monster moves there); the brain decision is covered by EditMode.
- Not changed (noted): clients show the default arrow length for the enemy bow (0.7 vs 0.8 m; the client resolves weapons from the player catalog); a refused shot still spends the brain's cooldown; the host blends the bow lowering to Idle earlier than clients (cosmetic).
