# D1b: combat flow (combo chains, impact zones, hit slow, move penalties, guard break, stamina removal, swap lock)

Product constraint: plays like Dark and Darker (own names, art, numbers). Builds on D1a (swept melee). Offline, authority-owned, server-runnable shape. Nothing is committed. User decisions applied: stamina removed from block and sprint, mana stays, shields never parry, six attributes kept. Coordinator addition: weapon swap is blocked during an attack.

Process note: the coordinator's resume message said the tree should be clean; it was not (this worker had already been implementing, nothing was reset). Work continued from the in-progress tree.

## Design and data formats

### 1. Combo chains (authority state, per instigator)
- `WeaponData.comboSteps[]` = `ComboStep {patternIndex, damageMultiplier, windupScale, recoveryScale}` (index into `swingPatterns`), `comboWindow`, `comboFinisherRecovery`.
- Sword: slash L->R 1.00, slash R->L 1.05 (windup x0.85), overhead 1.10; window 0.70 s; finisher +0.35 s recovery. Rapier: thrust x3 at 1.00/1.05/1.10 (steps 2-3 windup x0.9, step 2 recovery x0.9); window 0.60 s; finisher +0.30 s.
- `SwingPatternData.damageMultiplier` is now 1 for every pattern (the shape); the multiplier is the chain's. The overhead was 1.1 in D1a, so step 3 = base x 1.10 exactly.
- `SwingTiming` (windup/active/recovery/wallRecoil after step scales and finisher extra) is resolved once per swing and used by the authority sweep, the cooldown floor, the client and the weapon visual.
- Authority (`LocalCombatAuthority.Swings.cs`): `TryBeginMeleeSwing` validates step and pattern against the weapon chain, then against its OWN `ChainState{weapon, instigator, nextStep, windowEnd}`: step 0 always starts a fresh chain; step k>0 needs the chain of the same weapon, `k == nextStep`, `Now <= windowEnd` (windowEnd = swing start + windup + active + comboWindow, i.e. the window opens when the recovery begins). Reasons: "no running combo chain...", "combo step k is not the expected next step n", "combo window expired", "combo step k does not exist", "pattern p is not the chain's pattern q". All checks run before the sequence is stamped or the cooldown spent, so refusals change nothing.
- Chain resets: last step swung (finisher), window expiry, weapon swap (also forgotten on the next step if it happens between ticks), death (and begin while dead), vanished instigator, swing ended by anything but Completed or WallStop. A WALL STOP does not reset (review): it only cuts the swing short with the recoil, and the combo window restarts when the recoil ends (stop time + wallRecoil + comboWindow). A HIT TAKEN does NOT reset the chain (documented, gated). Chains are per instigator id; another entity cannot ride on it.
- `TryGetExpectedComboStep` lets the thin client ask the authority for the next step; `MeleeCombat` requests it with the chain's pattern (retries step 0 once if the authority disagrees), plays the visual for the accepted timing, and buffers a primary press made during the recovery for 0.5 s; the buffered press is spent only when the authority accepts the begin (a cooldown refusal keeps it). The sprint-overhead placeholder is gone.
- Found by the real-time smoke: a swing whose time is over was "in progress" until the next fixed step (the finisher's length equals the client cooldown). `TryBeginMeleeSwing` now steps the running swing to `Now` first.

### 2. Impact zones
`WeaponData.impactZones[]` = bands `{from, multiplier}` over the contact parameter t (0 hilt end, 1 tip). Sword: 0.7 / 0.9 (from 0.35) / 1.0 (from 0.70); rapier 0.7 / 0.9 (0.40) / 1.0 (0.65). The authority measures t from the sweep (closest point of the damage segment to the hit volume, along the unclipped segment) and carries it with the contact; damage = base x pattern x step x outgoing x location x zone. `MeleeSwingHit.ContactT/ZoneMultiplier` expose it.

### 3. Hit slow
`WeaponData.hitSlowMultiplier/hitSlowDuration` (sword 0.9 / 0.6 s, rapier 0.95 / 0.5 s, Warden maul 0.75 / 1.5 s). `ApplyHitEffects` puts a `StatusSpec(Slow)` on the accepted hit's target through `IStatusTarget` (R1b rule: from assets, never from an intent; the intent field list is pinned). `StatusComponent` already refreshes (max duration) and keeps the strongest slow, so no stacking. A fully blocked hit applies no slow, a broken-guard hit does. Works on players (rig), enemies (chase speed) and any `IStatusTarget`. Applied in both the swept path and the legacy claw `SubmitAttack` path.

### 4. Move penalties (`CombatFlowRules`, `ICombatFlowState`, `IMoveFlowSource`)
`PlayerMovement.ResolveGroundSpeed` multiplies by `PlayerRig.GetMoveFlowFactor()` = held factor x authority factor. Held: `1 - (weapon.heldMoveSpeedPenalty [+ a contributing off-hand shield's]) / 300` (floor 0.5). Authority factor: swing phase from the pattern (`windupMoveMultiplier` 0.85, `activeMoveMultiplier` 0.70, `recoveryMoveMultiplier` 0.85; wall recoil counts as recovery) on the authority clock, times the guard-stagger slow. Blocking stays `CharacterClassData.guardSpeedMultiplier`, now 0.65 for the Warrior (was 0.45). Dash and skills are untouched. `ResolveWalkSpeed` (the attribute rule) is unchanged.

### 5. Guard rework (no stamina)
`WeaponData.impactPower` (sword 10, rapier 6, claw 12, maul 30, bow and crossbow 0: arrows never break a guard) x `SwingPatternData.impactPowerScale` (overhead 1.2) goes into `DamageInfo.ImpactPower`; `impactResist` (heater shield 20, sword 8, rapier 5) feeds `DefenseProfile.ImpactResist` (best of the held weapon and a contributing shield). `PlayerRig` implements `IGuardBreakDefender`: same frontal arc (never from behind), then `CombatFlowRules.IsGuardBroken(power, resist)` = `power > 0 && power >= resist`. Broken: reduction x `flow.guardBreakReductionScale` (0.3), `DamageInfo.GuardBroken` + `Blocked`, the authority staggers the defender (`flow.guardStaggerSeconds` 0.8: `IsGuarding` is false, hits find no guard, move x0.6 from `flow.guardStaggerMoveMultiplier`). Skills carry no impact power and never break. HUD shows "GUARD BROKEN -N HP" through the existing damage event and toast. No parry anywhere (`DamageInfo` has no parry state; Sanitize still strips Parry from shields). `GameConfig.flow` (`CombatFlowTuning`) holds the three tunables (old config assets read the defaults).

### 6. Stamina removed (deprecated, nothing deleted)
Nothing spent stamina (verified by source scan in the gate and real measurement). The HUD bar is no longer built (`HudController.ShowStaminaBar = false`), the character panel's Stamina row is gone (`VitalRowCount` 3 -> 2). `StaminaComponent`, `CharacterClassData` fields, `AttributeRules` derived stamina and the gates' data assertions stay; marked DEPRECATED in `PlayerRig`/`HudController` comments and here.

### 7. Weapon swap lock (coordinator addition)
`ClassLoadout.IsActionLocked` = authority reports a melee swing in progress (windup, active, recovery) or the crossbow/bow is drawing. `SwitchToSlot`/`SwitchWeapon` refuse with no state change and a toast "Cannot swap weapons during an attack"; the swap input is only read on its frame, so nothing is queued. `ClassLoadout.TryReplaceWeaponSlot` (the inventory equip path) is locked too when it would replace the weapon in hand: `Equipment` refuses a main-hand weapon item mid-attack before it mutates anything ("cannot change weapons during an attack" + toast), and `TryReplaceWeaponSlot` refuses as the last line. `ForceSwitchToSlot` has NO runtime caller (gates only; class apply uses `Equip(0)` directly); it exists to prove the authority still ends a swing as `WeaponChanged` (defence in depth, gated). Reload is not locked (not requested).

## Rewritten fixtures (reasons; coverage preserved)

- `EmberdelveRuntimeSmokeTest` combat: "melee damage is weapon base x passive" read 19.6 vs 34.9 because the contact lands on a limb/zone factor (0.56). The follow-up "harder than the weapon alone" compares with that same predicted contact. realcombat matrices: a press that would be the sword's overhead (step 3) waits for the window to expire (the matrix pins the slashes); the rapier never waits.
- `EmberdelveAttributeActionGate`, `EmberdelveAttributeVitalsGate`, `EmberdelveNumericAffixGate`: warrior ground speeds now include the held factor (sword 10 + shield 8 = x0.94); expectations are rule number x held factor, `ResolveWalkSpeed` assertions untouched; guarded walk 4.3 x 0.65 (was 0.45).

## Open follow-ups
- Small items from the review, not done: `IsSwingInProgress` can lag one tick (it reads the record, not `Now`); an A-B-A weapon swap inside one frame can keep a chain; the held factor is applied after the walk clamp; per-entity dictionaries (`_chains`, `_guardBrokenUntil`, `_lastSwingFloor`) are never pruned; the new `EmberdelveInput` overrides are public (make internal later).
- Per-class/per-weapon `comboWindow` and the press buffer are first guesses; no combo pip on the HUD (optional, not done).
- Guard still client-owned (M-c from D1a): the authority does not refuse a swing begin while guarding; stagger is the only authority-owned guard state.
- Ranged weapons have no held penalty; ranged hits still use the old headshot rule; enemies are not on the swept system (D1a-2); only the Warden maul and claw author impact power.
- A buffered press and the block of weapon swap during reload are not covered; `ForceSwitchToSlot` callers (class apply, respawn) should be audited when networking lands.
- The D1a turn-rate cap (H2) is still open.
- Hit slow merge quirk (existing StatusComponent rule): a short hit slow onto a longer stronger skill slow keeps the stronger magnitude for the longer remaining time.
- StaminaComponent and derived stamina data are dead weight: remove in a later cleanup with the gates that assert them.
