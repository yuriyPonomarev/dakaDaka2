# R1c3 - validated dash, authority-driven casts, sender-less path closed

Status (27 Oct 2026): DONE. User decisions: dash = client moves, authority validates and rolls back; kit state ticked by the authority. Evidence Logs/r1c3_20261027_192629: MovementLifecycle 74/0, 52 of 53 groups PASS, EditMode 35/0; RangedLocation 99/0 after restoring its D1c3 fixture fixes lost in a stale draft copy (Logs/r1c3_rerun_20261027_200636).

Roadmap row: R1 follow-ups (kit epoch and sender ownership, authoritative movement ability execution). Third slice, on top of R1c1 (sender ownership) and R1c2 (authority-owned kit state). Uncompiled: no Unity here.

## Decisions applied
- The client moves; the authority validates the reported dash and rolls back a dishonest result. It does not execute movement.
- The sender-less overloads are refused for player-owned ids and stay trusted only for unowned ids (enemies, fixtures without an owner).
- A cast is completed or expired by the authority on its own clock.

## Changes
Dash validation (`Runtime/Combat/LocalCombatAuthority.Movement.cs`, new)
- `ICombatAuthority.ReportDashResult(sender, casterId, activationId, endPosition, out reason)`. True = accepted untouched.
- `IMovementRollback` (CombatContracts.cs): position, capsule centre offset and radius, `RollbackTo`. Implemented by `PlayerRig` over `PlayerMovement.TeleportTo` (controller off, write, on, SyncTransforms).
- `TryBeginSkillCast` of a Dash skill records start position, the clamped meters/seconds/speed (same clamps as `PlayerMovement.TryStartDash`, so an honest dash ends inside them) and the kit epoch.
- `PlayerMovement` raises `DashEnded(activationId, position)` once when a started dash ends (time out or wall); `PlayerRig` forwards it with its own sender. `DashBehavior` passes `context.ActivationId`. A cancelled dash (death) reports nothing.
- Checks, in order: sender owns the caster; activation is this caster's tracked dash, not yet reported, caster alive. Then the claim is judged by: distance (meters + 0.5 + 0.1 * speed), time (speed * min(elapsed since cast, duration) + same slack; "too early"), vertical (2.5 m), NaN, and a sphere cast along the path (radius 0.6 * capsule radius, centre height from the capsule, mask `levelMask`, hit distance + 0.3 slack).
- Dishonest: rig put back at the furthest honest point on the claimed heading (start height), `DashRollbackCount`, `LastDashRollbackReason`, `DashRollbacks` history (32), `DashRolledBack` event, diagnostics log. One report per activation; later reports are refused as "already reported".
- A dash nobody reports is judged by the authority itself `duration + 0.75 s` after the cast, from the entity's own position, with an extra allowance of 8 m/s for the time since the dash ended; skipped when the caster is dead or its kit epoch changed (death/respawn legitimately moves it).

Authority-driven completion
- `CompleteSkillCast` body moved to `CompleteActivation` (shared). `ExpireActivations()` runs on every authority tick (`AdvanceKits`), at the start of `TryBeginSkillCast` and `CompleteSkillCast`: a cast not completed by `OpenedAt + skillActivationWindow` (dash: max(window, duration + grace)) is completed by the authority; self buff applied once (`SelfStatusApplied` guard); dead caster gets nothing. Counter `ActivationsAutoCompleted`.
- The caller's prompt `CompleteSkillCast` is unchanged (buff after the hits, exactly once). A completion arriving after the window now finds the cast already closed (was: still accepted until window + 5 s).

Sender-less path
- `LegacyAllowed` at the head of each sender-less overload (SubmitAttack, TryBeginMeleeSwing, LaunchProjectile, TryBeginSkillCast, CompleteSkillCast, SubmitSkill, LaunchSkillProjectile). Passes for unowned ids, while a sender-bound overload is delegating (`_senderBound`, try/finally), or when the internal `AllowSenderlessPlayerCommands` seam is set (default false). Refusal: reason "<command> for player-owned entity #N carries no sender", `RejectedCount` and `SenderRefusalCount` up, nothing else touched.
- Fixtures: `EmberdelveKitFixtures.SenderFor(int combatId | PlayerRig)` plus extension wrappers `*AsOwner` (SubmitAttack, TryBeginMeleeSwing, LaunchProjectile, TryBeginSkillCast, CompleteSkillCast, SubmitSkill, LaunchSkillProjectile) that send as the owner of the intent's instigator (trusted host for unowned/unknown ids, so old rejection texts for unknown ids are unchanged).
- 95 call sites in 15 gates renamed `X.Method(` to `X.MethodAsOwner(` (Armor 1, AttributeAction 4, Cloak 1, CombatAuthority 6, CombatFlow 19, CombatHud 6, EnemySweep 1, FlatDamage 2, MultiPlayer 1, ProjectileBlock 8, RangedLocation 2, RuntimeSmoke 13, SkillAuthority 15, SweptMelee 15, TurnCeiling 1). KitState and SenderOwnership already pass explicit senders.

## Literals changed (old -> new, why)
- EmberdelveSenderOwnershipGate, "the sender-less overload keeps working for the rig's own id": expected `legacy && legacyAct > 0 && Complete(...)` -> expected `!legacy && legacyAct == 0 && reason has "no sender" && SenderRefusalCount + 1`. Reason: R1c3 closes exactly that path; the old expectation is the behaviour being removed.
- `ICombatAuthority.CompleteSkillCast` doc: "skipping this call only costs the caster its buff" -> authority completes it.
- No numeric expectation in any other gate changed. Fixtures that waited longer than the 1.5 s window between cast and completion would now see a closed cast: none found (SkillAuthority gate advances its injected clock only between separate casts).

## New gate: EmberdelveMovementLifecycleGate (+ .cs.meta, guid 7b3e91c4d5a64f08a2c1e6b9f0d37a58)
Entry `Emberdelve.EditorTools.EmberdelveMovementLifecycleGate.Run`; final line `[Emberdelve][MovementLifecycle] PASS|FAIL checks=N failures=M`; exits the editor. Covers: the real PlayerMovement dash accepted untouched; forged dash too far / through a wall / too early / climbing rolled back with event and position; honest dash stopped by a wall, a 95% dash and a non-moving dash untouched; other player, trusted host, replay, invented and non-dash activation reports refused with state unchanged; unreported dash judged by the authority (rolled back / left alone); never-completed Battle Cry completed once by the authority with its buff, late and repeated completion refused, prompt completion not doubled; sender-less cast, completion, swing, arrow and attack refused for a player with nothing spent and a following owner command working; an enemy's sender-less attack still works; helper resolves senders. Check count is not fixed by hand (about 75); the harness prints the real number.
New `.meta`: LocalCombatAuthority.Movement.cs.meta (guid e41f6a2b9c8d4073b5a0d19c2f86e347), both verified unique in the tree.

## Risks
- Uncompiled and never run. Highest risk spots: extension methods on `ICombatAuthority` in an `internal static class` used by public harness types (should be fine within the Editor assembly); the real-dash stage assumes `Spot` is open ground and Warrior has Dash in its kit.
- Tolerances (0.5 m + 0.1 * speed, 0.3 s-scale timing, wall slack 0.3 m, sphere radius 0.6 * capsule) are tuned by reasoning, not play-testing. Too tight rolls back honest dashes on frame spikes (the speed term allows about 100 ms); too loose lets a short cheat through. Ramps/stairs rely on the 2.5 m vertical bound only.
- The unreported-dash path gives 8 m/s of walking allowance, so a client that never reports can gain up to that much; the normal path reports at dash end.
- Dash rollback teleports the CharacterController; a dash that legitimately ended on a moving platform is not modelled.
- Anything outside the repo that calls the sender-less overloads for a player id (tools, future tests) is refused now; `AllowSenderlessPlayerCommands` is the internal escape hatch, off by default.
- A caller that completed a cast later than 1.5 s after opening used to still get its buff; now the authority applies it at the window end. Same buff, slightly earlier.
